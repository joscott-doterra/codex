# Teenager Translator Skill

## When to use this skill

Use this skill when:
- User asks what a slang term means
- User wants to translate text into teenager speak
- User needs help understanding messages from teens
- User wants to sound more relatable to younger audiences

## Translation Modes

### Mode 1: Slang to English (Decode)

Translate teenager slang into plain English that older generations understand.

**Input:** "No cap, that movie was lowkey fire. The main character had me dead fr fr. My friend was salty cuz she thought it was mid but she's just being extra."

**Output:** "I'm being completely honest, that movie was surprisingly really good. The main character made me laugh so hard. My friend was upset because she thought it was mediocre but she's just overreacting."

### Mode 2: English to Slang (Encode)

Translate plain English into teenager speak.

**Input:** "I'm very tired and don't want to go to the party. My outfit looks really good though. I'll probably stay home and relax."

**Output:** "I'm lowkey dead and not tryna go to the party. My fit is bussin tho no cap. I'll prolly just chill at home and vibe."

## Translation Guidelines

### When Decoding (Slang → English)
1. Identify slang terms in the input
2. Replace with plain English equivalents
3. Maintain the original tone and intent
4. Add context if meaning is ambiguous
5. Keep sentence structure natural

### When Encoding (English → Slang)
1. Identify key emotions and descriptors
2. Replace with appropriate slang
3. Add filler words naturally (lowkey, fr, ngl)
4. Don't overdo it - real teens don't use slang in every word
5. Match intensity - casual vs enthusiastic

## Examples

### Example 1: Decode
**User:** "What does 'that's giving NPC energy' mean?"

**Response:** "This means someone is acting like a non-player character from a video game - they seem scripted, boring, or like they're just going through the motions without any personality or original thoughts."

### Example 2: Encode
**User:** "Translate to teen speak: I really enjoyed the concert last night. The artist performed very well and the crowd was enthusiastic."

**Response:** "Ngl the concert last night was fire. The artist absolutely ate and left no crumbs fr fr. The crowd was hype, everyone was vibing. No cap it was a whole vibe, 10/10 would go again."

### Example 3: Conversation Decode
**User:** "My daughter texted me 'the fit is giving but the venue is mid, might dip early ngl'. What?"

**Response:** "Translation: 'My outfit looks really good, but the venue/location is mediocre. I might leave early, not gonna lie.' She's saying she dressed well but isn't impressed with wherever she is and may come home soon."

## Tips for Natural Translation

### Do:
- Use slang contextually
- Keep some normal words
- Match the energy of the original
- Use abbreviations in casual contexts

### Don't:
- Replace every single word with slang
- Mix incompatible slang eras
- Use slang incorrectly (worse than not using it)
- Force slang where it doesn't fit

## Cultural Notes

- Slang evolves rapidly - terms can become "cheugy" (outdated) quickly
- Different communities have different slang
- Some terms originate from AAVE (African American Vernacular English)
- TikTok and social media accelerate slang spread
- Using outdated slang is worse than using no slang
